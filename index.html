Seven Wonders of the World

Great Wall of China (China)

It was build by Qin Shihuang the first Emperor of China. This is the man made structure that can be seen from the space. It was build to protect China from invading Huns, Mongals and other tribes.

Petra (Ma'an Jordan)

Petra is located in Ma'an Jordan. It was made in 1200 BC. It is known as the Rose City due to colour of the stone. It remained unknown to the western world until 1812, hence it is known as the "Lost City of Petra". Petra is a popular tourist destinatio in Jordan. It was the filming set for a number of famous movies such as The Mummy Returns & Transformers.

Christ the Redeemer (Rio de Janeiro,Brazil)

It is located in Rio De Janeiro, Brazil. Christ the Redeemer is a big statue of Jesus Christ. It is 30m tall and arms are 28m wide. It was made from 60,000 pieces of stones. About 100 people contributed to make it.

Machu Picchu(Cuzco Region,Peru)

It is located in Peru. Built by Incan emperor. It lies deep in the Amazon Jungle. It symbolises the excellent technical skills and productivity of the Inca empire.

Chichen Itza(Yucat'an,Mexico)

It is located in Yucatan, Mexico. Built in 9th and 12th centuries by Maya People. The temple has 365 steps one for each day of the year, each side of temple has 91 steps and the top platform makes the 365th. Chichen Itza means at the mouth of the well of the Itza. t is the most visited archaeological sites in Mexico.

Colosseum(Rome,Italy)

The colosseum is located in Rome, Italy. Built in 70-80AD. Built by Empire Vespasian, made from stone and concrete. The Colosseum is the largest amphitheatre, located in the centre of Rome. It is kind of sport stadium, In ancient times wild animals and prisoners were held there.

Taj Mahal(Agra,Uttar Pradesh,India)

It is located in Agra, India It is largest tourist attraction in India. Built in 21 Years (1632-1653) Built by Muslim Mughal Empire "Shah Jahan" memorial for his beloved wife 'Mumtaz". Taj is an elegant structure made of white marble. It is widely recognised as 'The jewel of muslim art" in India.
